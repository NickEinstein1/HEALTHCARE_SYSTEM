Pharmacy Dashboard Manual

Dispensing queue, verification, stock, KEMSA orders, and clinical pharmacy tools.

Audience: Pharmacists & dispensing staff URL: /dashboard/pharmacy Version: Pilot 2026

1. Sign-in — Pharmacy

Pharmacy staff sign in with Pharmacy Registration ID, email, password, and MFA.

Step 1 — Select role

The Pharmacy tile is highlighted in the role carousel. The audit panel logs authentication events for compliance.

Step 2 — Enter credentials

Enter Pharmacy Registration ID (e.g. PHARM-00441), email, and password.

Step 3 — Verify MFA

Enter the six-digit OTP sent to your institutional email. In staging/demo, use Practice 360 or OTP 123456.

Step 4 — Launch dashboard

Confirms Pharmacy — Clinical Dispensing. Opens dispensing workstation.

6. Tips & troubleshooting

Recommended workflow

  1. Review Overview and Prescription Queue.
  2. Verify & Approve each Rx — check interactions.
  3. Dispense and update stock levels.
  4. Process KEMSA reorders before cutoff.

Common issues

IssueAction
Rx queue emptyConfirm clinician orders reached /pharmacy API
Stock mismatchRun stock reconcile; check audit trail
Interaction alertContact prescriber before overriding
Patient safety: Never bypass verification for look-alike/sound-alike drugs.
