FULL_DESCRIPTION="""
UniHealth 360 is the official patient and member app for the UniHealth national 
health platform. Manage your Social Health Authority (SHA) coverage, track claims, 
pay your SHIF premium via M-Pesa, and stay on top of your family's health benefits 
— all from your phone.

WHAT YOU CAN DO:

Coverage & Benefits
• View your active SHIF, PHF, and ECCIF coverage status in real time
• See exactly how much of each fund you have used this year
• Check your monthly premium due date and amount
• View all registered dependants (spouse, children, parents)

Claims
• See every claim filed at any hospital on your behalf
• Track claim status: submitted, approved, paid, or rejected
• Understand exactly what was covered and what you owe
• Download and share your Explanation of Benefits (EOB) as a PDF
• Raise a dispute directly from the app if a claim is rejected

Premium Payments
• Pay your monthly SHIF contribution via M-Pesa STK Push
• Real-time payment confirmation within 30 seconds
• Full payment history with receipts

Dependants
• Add or remove household dependants
• SHA verification status shown per dependant
• Up to 5 dependants supported per household

Documents
• Upload supporting documents for claims (PDF, JPG, PNG)
• Store your National ID, disability certificates, and referral letters
• All documents encrypted and retained per Digital Health Act 2023

Profile & Consent
• Manage which healthcare providers can access your data
• Control your treatment, billing, and research consent separately
• Biometric login (fingerprint / Face ID) for fast, secure access

DESIGNED FOR:
All Kenyan residents registered under the Social Health Authority (SHA) and the 
National Social Health Insurance Fund (SHIF), as well as members of licensed 
private health insurance plans operating on the UniHealth 360 platform.

SECURITY & PRIVACY:
• Biometric + PIN authentication
• All data encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256)
• Compliant with Kenya's Data Protection Act 2019, Digital Health Act 2023,
  and Social Health Insurance Act 2023
• Data hosted in Kenya — no cross-border transfer without explicit consent
• ODPC-registered data controller

HELPLINE: 0800 720 601 (SHA toll-free)
"""
